Privacy policy

Obsidian Calendar is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

  • Google Calendar events are read and written directly between Google and the owner's local Obsidian app.
  • No data is sent to, stored on, or shared with any server operated by this project or any third party.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no sale of data.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
